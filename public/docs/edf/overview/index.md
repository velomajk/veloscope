# Overview

Epic Delivery Forecast turns the issues under your epics into one realistic completion date. It combines your real-time Jira data — epics and their child issues — with your team's historical velocity to predict when work will actually be done.

## Introduction

Epic Delivery Forecast is a Jira dashboard gadget. Point it at a project and a Fix Version, give it your team's velocity, and it rolls every story point of remaining work into a single number: how many sprints you still need. No exports, no spreadsheets, no separate login.

> [!info] Read-only by design. The gadget only reads your Jira data to compute the forecast — it never modifies issues, fields, or workflows.

## Key features

- **Epic-level visibility** — see all epics in a specific Fix Version at a glance.
- **Data-driven forecasts** — automatically calculates "Sprints Required" from remaining story points.
- **Dynamic velocity** — connects to your Scrum board to use a real 5-sprint average, or accepts a manual override for scenario planning.
- **Health signals** — clear indicators for unestimated work and blocking issues that put your date at risk.
