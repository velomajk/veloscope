What it does

Per-issue KUP recordsRecord the reporting month and eligible KUP hours directly on configured Jira issues.
Monthly working-hour baselinesCompare recorded KUP hours with the monthly baseline configured for your organisation.
Absence and overtime adjustmentsAdjust an employee’s effective monthly working-hour base when needed.
KUP limit visibilitySee the monthly KUP percentage and flag records that exceed the configured company limit.
Manager approvalManagers can review, approve, or unapprove monthly KUP records. Approved records are locked from further edits.
Payroll exportsGenerate a monthly CSV or XLSX summary for your payroll workflow.

How it works

1 Configure eligibility An administrator selects eligible Jira projects and issue types, reporting months, baselines, limits, and manager access.
2 Record and review Employees record eligible KUP hours on Jira issues and review their monthly totals. Managers review team reports and adjustments.
3 Approve and export Managers approve completed records, then generate a monthly payroll summary in CSV or XLSX format.
